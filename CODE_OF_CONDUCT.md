# Code of Conduct - RoomMate Match

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- **Respect:** Treat others with respect and professionalism
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome and support diverse perspectives
- **Collaboration:** Work together constructively
- **Empathy:** Consider the impact of your words and actions
- **Open-mindedness:** Be receptive to different viewpoints

### Unacceptable Behavior
- **Harassment:** Unwelcome comments, jokes, or advances
- **Discrimination:** Prejudiced comments or actions
- **Disrespect:** Dismissive or hostile behavior
- **Intimidation:** Threats or personal attacks
- **Exclusion:** Deliberate exclusion of individuals

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, Discord, Slack)
- Public and private communications
- In-person events and meetings
- All project contributors and users

## Reporting Guidelines

### How to Report
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to:

**Email:** conduct@roommatematch.com

**What to Include:**
- Description of the incident
- Individuals involved (if known)
- Context and timeline
- Any evidence (screenshots, logs)

### Confidentiality
All reports will be handled confidentially. We will protect the privacy of all parties involved.

### Response Process
1. **Acknowledgment:** Response within 48 hours
2. **Investigation:** Thorough review of the incident
3. **Action:** Appropriate measures taken
4. **Follow-up:** Resolution communicated to reporter

## Enforcement

### Consequences
Unacceptable behavior may result in:
- **Warning:** Formal notice of unacceptable behavior
- **Temporary Suspension:** Limited access to project resources
- **Permanent Ban:** Removal from project community
- **Legal Action:** For severe violations

### Appeal Process
If you believe enforcement action was unjustified:
- Submit appeal to conduct@roommatematch.com
- Include evidence and context
- Review within 7 business days

## Community Guidelines

### Communication
- **Be Constructive:** Focus on solutions, not problems
- **Be Clear:** Use clear and respectful language
- **Be Patient:** Allow time for responses and understanding
- **Be Professional:** Maintain professional demeanor

### Collaboration
- **Share Knowledge:** Help others learn and grow
- **Give Credit:** Acknowledge contributions of others
- **Be Open:** Accept feedback gracefully
- **Be Inclusive:** Welcome new community members

### Development
- **Follow Standards:** Adhere to coding standards
- **Document Changes:** Maintain clear documentation
- **Test Thoroughly:** Ensure quality contributions
- **Review Carefully:** Participate in code reviews

## Diversity and Inclusion

### Commitment
We are committed to:
- **Diverse Representation:** Encourage participation from underrepresented groups
- **Inclusive Environment:** Create welcoming spaces for all
- **Equal Opportunity:** Provide equal opportunities for contribution
- **Continuous Improvement:** Regularly assess and improve our practices

### Resources
- **Mentorship:** Support for new contributors
- **Accessibility:** Ensure accessible communication channels
- **Language:** Support multiple languages when possible
- **Time Zones:** Consider global community in scheduling

## Conflict Resolution

### Principles
- **Direct Communication:** Address issues directly when possible
- **Mediation:** Seek mediation for unresolved conflicts
- **Escalation:** Escalate to leadership when necessary
- **Documentation:** Document conflicts and resolutions

### Process
1. **Direct Discussion:** Attempt to resolve directly
2. **Mediation:** Request mediation from project maintainers
3. **Formal Report:** Submit formal report if unresolved
4. **Decision:** Accept final decision of project leadership

## Project Leadership Responsibilities

### Commitment
Project leaders commit to:
- **Enforcement:** Fair and consistent enforcement
- **Transparency:** Clear communication of decisions
- **Education:** Provide guidance on conduct
- **Support:** Support victims of misconduct

### Accountability
Project leaders are held to the same standards as all community members.
